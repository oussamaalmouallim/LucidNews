# Health and pharma news profile

Use this profile for timely developments in health and pharma: decisions and safety alerts of health authorities, medicine and vaccine approvals, shortages and recalls, clinical trial results, medical devices, public health and epidemics, digital health, biotech, and the organisation of hospitals and care.

Typical items include a publication of a health authority (approval, opinion, safety alert, recommendation), a peer-reviewed study or trial result, surveillance data, a change in pricing or reimbursement, or a significant development at a pharmaceutical, biotech or medtech company.

Do not use this profile for medical advice, wellness or diet tips, miracle-cure claims, product promotion, sponsored content, or personal health stories without news value.
