# Energy and climate news profile

Use this profile for timely developments in energy and climate: energy prices and supply, renewables, energy efficiency and white certificate schemes (CEE in France), energy renovation of buildings, grids and storage, energy laws and regulator decisions, climate policy, emissions and corporate sustainability reporting, and the energy transition of transport.

Typical items include a published law, decree, order or regulator decision, a new or revised standardised operation sheet, a tariff or price change, a tender result, grid or supply data from a system operator, an official climate or emissions report, or a significant development at an energy company.

Do not use this profile for investment tips, energy price predictions presented as facts, product promotion, sponsored content, or opinion pieces without news value.
