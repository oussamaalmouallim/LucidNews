# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely finance news for professionals who follow markets, banks, insurers, crypto-assets, deals, the economy and financial regulation, the way a careful analyst would.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Major.** A central bank rate decision, a systemic bank or insurer in trouble, a new law or rule that changes how a whole market works, a market-wide shock, or a deal that reshapes an industry.
- **7-8: High value.** A supervisor's decision or sanction against a named firm, key economic statistics, results or a deal of a large listed company, a final rule or official guidance.
- **5-6: Interesting.** A consultation, a draft rule, a mid-sized deal, sector data, or a well-sourced analysis tied to a recent event.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine notices, minor appointments, daily price moves with no stated cause, repeated coverage of an older event.
- **0-2: Noise.** Investment tips, price predictions, promotional or sponsored content, off-topic material.

# Evaluation guidance

Favour primary sources: a central bank, a supervisor, an official statistics office, or the company's own filing outrank a commentary on them. Judge the size of the effect (who is affected, how much money, which market), not the drama of the headline. A forecast, an analyst's target or an opinion is never a fact: score it as an opinion and say whose it is. Never reward a call to buy, sell or hold. A figure counts only if the source gives it.

Use three to five specific topic tags (institutions, instruments, markets or rules named in the item).
