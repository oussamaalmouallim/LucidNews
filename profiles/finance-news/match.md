# Finance news profile

Use this profile for timely developments in financial markets, banking, insurance, crypto-assets, mergers and acquisitions, the economy, financial regulation and fintech.

Typical items include decisions and publications of central banks and financial supervisors, economic statistics, company results and deals with a material financial effect, enforcement actions and sanctions, new financial rules, and market moves explained by a stated cause.

Do not use this profile for investment tips, price predictions without a named source, promotional offers, personal finance advice, sponsored content, or market chatter that reports a price move without any cause or consequence.
