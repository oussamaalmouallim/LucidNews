# Legal and regulatory news profile

Use this profile for timely developments in law and regulation: laws, decrees and orders, bills and their progress, court and constitutional decisions, EU legal acts and Court of Justice rulings, decisions and sanctions of regulators such as data protection authorities, and new compliance obligations for organisations.

Typical items include an official publication (an official journal, a court's own decision page, an authority's press release), the adoption or rejection of a text, a ruling that settles a disputed question, a sanction with its legal basis, or a consultation on a draft rule.

Do not use this profile for legal advice, law firm marketing, event announcements, job offers, general political news without a legal act, or crime reports that only tell the facts of a case.
